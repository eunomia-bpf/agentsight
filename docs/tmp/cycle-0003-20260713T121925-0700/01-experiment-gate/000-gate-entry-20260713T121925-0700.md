## Specific Uncertainty

The current paper claims that operation-stack profiling locates annotated
problems while inspecting little work. Its visible RQ2 table does not establish
that outcome at matched recall: operation-stack AP is below per-session and
native-hierarchy AP, the 9.4% work point accompanies only 18.8% median top-five
recall, and configuration was adapted on the evaluated tasks.

The decisive uncertainty is therefore:

> When targets are official, fresh, step-level, and excluded from all method
> construction, does semantic responsibility grouping reduce atomic-step
> inspection at the same 80% macro recall relative to the strongest comparator
> with identical visible information?

This is a direct test of the paper's real-problem-localization consequence,
not a proxy for group count, AP alone, run detection, or infrastructure
readiness.

## Paper-Value Admission

### Largest credible paper story unlocked

A positive result would show that agent profiling changes a real diagnostic
decision: across a fresh external population with official risky-step labels,
developers can recover a fixed fraction of real risky behavior while reading
less trajectory work than they would under source order, a flat step ranker, or
ordinary same-information aggregation. That result would turn “profiling, not
only debugging” from an attractive analogy into a measurable population-level
localization advantage.

The claim remains inside RQ2 and does not replace the broader four-RQ paper.

### Strongest reject argument addressed

The experiment attacks the largest reader-visible reject argument:

> Current AgentProf gains are explained by curated fields plus task-specific
> ranking or ordinary multidimensional aggregation, while low inspection work
> is obtained by accepting low recall.

Matched 80% macro recall removes the low-recall explanation. Same-information
baselines remove the extra-field explanation. Fresh HINTBench targets remove
the target-reuse explanation.

### Independent evidence beyond prior runs

- HINTBench targets have not appeared in an earlier AgentProf experiment or
  result path.
- The current official snapshot contains 536 raw trajectories, including 400
  risky and 136 safe cases.
- It supplies 978 official annotations over 938 distinct target-step pairs;
  935 pairs match released steps and three fixed official targets are absent
  from their released trajectories.
- AgentRx and TELBench cannot serve as fresh confirmation because cycle 0001
  already used their complete released target populations.
- AgentProcessBench cannot be retuned again because two constructions have
  already used the same observed targets.
- AgentTelemetry is a fault-detection/OTel precedent but its accepted artifact
  has no official localization gold.

This source therefore adds a new target population, not another score on an
observed benchmark.

### Why the experiment is not tautological or already settled

Official target identities enter only scoring after target-blind outputs
exist. AgentProf and main baselines receive the same raw steps, allowed
non-label fields, target-blind scores, and tuning opportunity. The primary
metric fixes recall before comparing work. Grouping by the gold target or
defining correctness from AgentProf output is forbidden.

Prior runs do not settle the question. They produced positive semantic AP
specificity but inconclusive work effects, or tested a different mechanism,
source, target, or information boundary.

## Competing Positions For Plan Review

Plan review must admit the smallest set that represents these load-bearing
positions:

1. **Native sequential inspection:** trajectory order is sufficient.
2. **Flat independent-step ranking:** the target-blind score creates any
   benefit; grouping does not.
3. **Flat same-information multidimensional aggregation:** ordinary grouping
   over every field visible to AgentProf captures any useful concentration;
   ordered stacks add nothing.

These are baselines inside one integrated matrix, not separate experiments.
Known-weak rows, oracles, and extra prompt variants are optional controls only
when needed to interpret one of these strong positions.

## Primary Decision And Completion Boundary

For each risky trajectory, the target set is the union of official target step
IDs. Duplicate risk annotations on one step count once. The primary metric is
the minimum atomic-step inspection fraction and count needed to reach at least
80% macro recall across the 400 risky trajectories.

The result supports the hypothesis only when AgentProf reaches the recall
target and the paired work-reduction interval excludes zero against the
strongest admitted same-information non-oracle baseline.
