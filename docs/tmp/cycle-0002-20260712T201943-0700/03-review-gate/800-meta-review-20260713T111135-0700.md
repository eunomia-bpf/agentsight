### Tested hypothesis

On disjoint held-out AgentTelemetry fault families and held-out agent
frameworks, a target-blind AgentProf semantic operation profile reduces the
fraction and weighted amount of official fault-bearing operations inspected to
reach 80% macro recall, relative to the strongest same-information non-oracle
baseline, while meeting the same recall threshold.

This is a tested hypothesis inside fixed RQ2. It does not modify the paper-level
RQ, thesis, original story, or RQ3/RQ4 hypotheses.

### Required scientific shape

1. **Real source eligibility.** Use the official AgentTelemetry artifact and
   its published protocol. The real preflight must establish that official
   span/step targets or first-anomaly identities exist at the claimed
   localization unit. Run-level-only labels are insufficient for this selected
   experiment and may not be replaced by handmade labels.
2. **Target blindness and held-out transfer.** Define fields, tagger or mapping,
   operation-stack order, ranker, fixed-recall metric, and success criterion in
   the ordinary Markdown plan before inspecting held-out target values. Keep
   development and test fault families and frameworks disjoint.
3. **Smallest strong same-information comparison.** Include the native
   trace/per-session view, the strongest equal-information conventional
   multidimensional aggregation available over every field AgentProf sees, and
   AgentTelemetry's strongest applicable non-oracle analysis. An oracle may be
   reported only as an upper bound. Do not give AgentProf extra target identity,
   fields, tuning, or evaluation budget.
4. **Primary decision.** Measure operations/spans and weighted inspection work
   needed to reach 80% macro recall of official fault targets. The result
   supports the tested hypothesis only if the paired uncertainty interval for
   work reduction excludes zero against the strongest same-information
   non-oracle baseline while the recall target is met. A lower-work result
   caused by lower recall is not support.
5. **Complete execution.** After one real preflight establishes eligibility,
   run every approved held-out fault-by-framework cell to terminal status and
   report per-family/per-framework heterogeneity and uncertainty. Do not stop at
   a smoke run and do not start a second experiment based on the sign.
6. **RQ boundary.** Do not add cold/warm profile construction cost or claim an
   RQ4 answer. Do not add tagger-accuracy evaluation as an RQ3 program. Any
