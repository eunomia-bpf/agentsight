## Whole-Paper Scientific Verdict

### Recommendation

**Reject for AAAI-27 Main Track in the current form, with high confidence;
incomplete-but-promising.**

The problem and principle are strong enough to preserve. The current paper is
not submission-ready because the causal evidence chain is incomplete, not
because the idea is too large or too ambitious.

### Plain-language principle

Treat accumulated agent runs as profiling samples: preserve additive measured
effects and organize them under recurring semantic responsibility so that
population-level resource and failure patterns become inspectable.

### Challenged belief

Application-level traces and dashboards are sufficient even when
responsibility crosses prompts, model calls, tools, processes, and OS effects.

### Strongest alternative explanation

Conventional multidimensional trace aggregation over curated fields plus a
task-specific ranker explains the current gains. The paper has not yet isolated
an advantage from cross-layer responsibility reconstruction or from operation-
stack organization itself.

### Why the paper is still promising

The principle is simple, durable, and consequential. It bridges a mature
profiling idea with a real agent-observability problem. A conserved semantic
responsibility record and a real matched-recall localization win could make the
paper simple-but-deep. Current implementation breadth shows feasibility, but
the load-bearing correctness and decision outcomes still need evidence.

## Paper-Visible Blockers

### RQ1: resource attribution

The displayed separation metric partly scores the same category inserted into
the grouping key. Adding `prompt_tag` mechanically removes prompt-tag mixing;
it does not independently validate semantic responsibility for process, file,
network, tool, or model effects. The “separates over 90%” headline is not
reconciled with the displayed 90.4% to 36.7% prompt-only change. Independent
lineage and attribution truth remain missing.

### RQ2: real-problem localization

The current Table 1 operation-stack AP is 0.312, below per-session 0.348 and
native hierarchy 0.357. The 9.4% work headline accompanies only 18.8% median
top-five recall. Fields, mappings, ranking, and depth were also adapted on the
same operations. This is not a matched-recall localization win.

### RQ3: tag accuracy

RQ3 evaluates structured-field remapping into phases and uses partition
agreement. It does not test the load-bearing regex/local-LLM prompt tagger,
semantic label correctness, OOS behavior, coverage, stability, or downstream
inheritance. V-measure cannot by itself authorize natural-language tag
accuracy.

### RQ4: profiling cost

The 1.6-second number measures cached projection/folding configurations while
the paper separately reports 35,136 local-LLM tag calls without complete cold-
path wall time and resource measurement. Cached folding is useful evidence but
not the full profiling-cost answer.

### Novelty and status quo

The operation-stack model is an ordered field projection plus additive
aggregation. Data Cube, Pivot Tracing, PerfettoSQL, pprof tag frames, Datadog
Patterns, Langfuse/LangSmith, aggregate traces, and AgentTelemetry create strong
same-mechanism or same-capability pressure. AgentProf must demonstrate its
cross-layer semantic responsibility and decision outcome, not rely on the
flame-graph shape or categorical claims that population semantic metrics do not
exist.

## Exactly One Next Experiment

### Assigned RQ

**RQ2: Does Profiler Output Correspond to Real Problems?**

### Tested hypothesis

Across the complete current official HINTBench test snapshot, grouping
target-blind atomic trajectory steps by stable semantic responsibility and
ranking the resulting groups requires less atomic-step inspection to recover
at least 80% of official risky-step targets than the strongest comparator that
sees exactly the same raw steps and non-label fields.

### Primary metric and success

For each risky trajectory, targets are the union of official step IDs;
duplicate annotations on one step count once. The primary metric is the minimum
atomic-step inspection fraction and count needed to reach at least 80% macro
recall across the 400 risky trajectories. Support requires reaching that recall
and a paired work-reduction interval excluding zero against the strongest
same-information non-oracle comparator.

### Strong competing positions

The plan-review loop admits the smallest set covering:

1. native sequential inspection: order alone is sufficient;
2. flat independent-step ranking: the target-blind score alone creates the
   benefit; and
3. flat same-information multidimensional aggregation: ordinary grouping over
   all fields visible to AgentProf captures the benefit without ordered stacks.

These are baseline positions inside one experiment, not separate experiments.
Every method receives the same raw steps, allowed non-label fields, target-
blind scores, and tuning opportunity.
