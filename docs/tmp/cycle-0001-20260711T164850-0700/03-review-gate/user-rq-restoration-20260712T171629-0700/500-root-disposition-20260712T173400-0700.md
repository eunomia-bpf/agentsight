## Disposition

### Accept: restore the original four-RQ academic architecture

The paper and current frontier must use exactly these four questions and keep
them separate:

1. **RQ1: Does Semantic Profiling Improve Resource Attribution?**
2. **RQ2: Does Profiler Output Correspond to Real Problems?**
3. **RQ3: How Accurate Are the Tags?**
4. **RQ4: What Is the Profiling Cost?**

The previous three-RQ organization is superseded. Fidelity, matched information,
native baselines, transfer, limitations, and total-cost accounting remain
important experimental controls; they are not replacement RQs.

### Accept: restore a positive hypothesis under every RQ

- RQ1 expects semantic operation stacks to reunite recurring responsibility
  fragmented across executions and thereby improve attribution of independently
  recorded additive measures.
- RQ2 expects the resulting profile to concentrate real failures, regressions,
  unsafe effects, or wasted work, reduce inspection, and ideally guide a
  successful held-out intervention.
- RQ3 expects a target-blind fixed tagger or mapping to provide accurate and
  stable identities on unseen agents and task families.
- RQ4 expects complete profiling to have practical predictable cost and cheap
  repeated queries through reuse and caching.

These are bold falsifiable hypotheses, not permission to invent results. The
root may improve the mechanism, source signal, workload, protocol, implementation,
or experiment design to test them. A failed constructor or workload cannot by
itself change the RQ or hypothesis.

### Accept: restore the strongest simple story

The paper's center is the missing population-level profiling capability:

> **Agent observability needs profiling, not only debugging.**

Agent trajectories are profiling samples. Operations preserve weighted recorded
activity; operation stacks attribute measures to recurring responsibility;
profiles expose what to optimize, inspect, or constrain. This is more direct,
consequential, and attractive than a paper centered on representation choice,
fairness controls, or two failed hierarchy mechanisms.

Operations and operation stacks remain the only core abstractions. Flat,
source-native, and semantic projections remain controls. Taggers, mappings,
rankers, differential comparison, intervention, pprof, and flamegraphs remain
supporting techniques.

### Accept: move failed intermediate experiments out of the paper's current story

AgentRx/TELBench and the recursive Hodoscope adapter remain fully preserved in
timestamped result reports and the detailed experiment history. They prohibit
reusing those unchanged mechanisms as if they succeeded. They do not define the
final paper and should not remain in the Abstract, Introduction, Contributions,
main Evaluation result story, Related Work summary, or Conclusion once WRITE
expresses this disposition.

This is not permission to hide a contradiction to the final method. The next
paper evidence must use a materially improved mechanism and a profiling-native
signal. Until positive experiments finish, the paper uses explicit result TODOs
or labels the draft as an active research program; it does not restore the old
unaudited numbers.

### Reject

- retaining three RQs because cost is “premature”;
- replacing tag accuracy with a broad transfer/limits question;
- making flat/native/semantic representation choice the thesis;
- making negative intermediate mechanisms the empirical contribution;
- restoring original positive values before clean experiments authorize them;
- adding a new named abstraction, selector, contract, or hierarchy system to
  create novelty;
- stopping at a smoke test, toy trace, private script-only workload, or
  retrospective ranking without an engineering decision.

## Why This Is Stronger Than Both Prior Narratives

The Initial Narrative is strongest in importance, simplicity, memorability, and
the complete attribution-localization-tag-cost chain. It is weaker only because
its evidence did not justify several positive statements.

The immediately previous three-RQ narrative is strongest in experimental
discipline: it established conservation-versus-lineage, accounting-versus-
causation, genuine native baselines, label timing, and matched evidence. It is
weaker as a paper because it turns controls into questions, drops tag accuracy,
subordinates cost, and lets failed mechanisms replace the positive research
program.

The accepted narrative restores the Initial Narrative's stronger academic
architecture and uses the later controls solely to produce trustworthy positive
evidence. It is therefore more faithful and more attractive than the previous
version without returning to unsupported claims.

## Experiment Program And Search Branch

Each RQ now has one complete evidence package:

- RQ1: a real agent/official benchmark with native spans and independent
  AgentSight process/tool lineage; compare semantic attribution with flat,
  session, and genuine native views.
- RQ2: a real published before/after additive regression; compare identical
  operations through flat, native, and one fixed semantic profile; use the top
  frame to choose an intervention and confirm the regression falls on held-out
  tasks without unacceptable success loss.
- RQ3: one fixed tag vocabulary and target-blind tagger/mapping; leave out both
  family and, when possible, agent/model; measure semantic accuracy, boundaries,
  coverage, stability, and downstream attribution consequence.
- RQ4: release-build end-to-end profiling cost over complete naturally occurring
  corpora, with cold/warm caches, stage costs, CPU, peak RSS, model use, storage,
  output, and capture overhead when used.

The next empirical branch remains RQ2 because a successful intervention most
directly proves the thesis and supplies the strongest paper headline. Reopen
`research-literature-novelty` after WRITE to identify a released agent and
official benchmark with a real additive regression and source-native hierarchy.
SWE-agent/SWE-bench is a candidate family, not yet an approved pair.

## Hypothesis Revisit Threshold

A local negative result, failed preflight, unavailable field, reviewer objection,
or one/two mechanism failures never changes a fixed RQ. The root continues with
the most promising improved mechanism and real source. A hypothesis can be
declared genuinely impossible only after repeated independent, complete,
prespecified real-world tests of the final intended capability eliminate every
plausible signal/mechanism explanation and directly falsify the hypothesis.
Even then, the fixed thesis remains; the next action is a bolder mechanism
replacement, such as intervention-derived responsibility, rather than a smaller
paper.
