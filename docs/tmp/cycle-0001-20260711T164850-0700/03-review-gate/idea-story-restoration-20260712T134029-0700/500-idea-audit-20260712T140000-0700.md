## Disposition

**Accept a combined restoration.** Restore the original paper-level center:
agent observability needs profiling across recurring behavior and measured
effects, not only tracing or debugging individual executions. Retain the
current version's scientific corrections: source-native hierarchy is real
evidence and a strong baseline; no semantic hierarchy is automatically correct;
accounting, diagnosis, and causality are distinct; and all admitted negative
results remain visible.

**Reject representation choice as the thesis.** It remains a load-bearing
property of the profiling model and a central evaluation dimension, but it
cannot replace the consequential profiling problem. The AgentRx/TELBench and
Hodoscope results test particular hypotheses and workloads. They do not directly
challenge the paper-level thesis that cross-run profiling of recurring measured
behavior complements tracing.

**Retain three RQs.** RQ1 covers faithful representation and reprojection, RQ2
covers real analytical value for cost/regression/safety/failure, and RQ3 covers
transfer and limits. They remain within the user-fixed 2–5 range and are not
silently narrowed.

## Why This Is Better Than Initial And Previous Narratives

The initial narrative is better at problem importance, memorable distinction,
and conceptual economy. It is worse at scientific authorization: it treated
prompt categories as an oracle, used hidden positives in ranking, understated
native trace structure and prior mechanisms, and promoted unsupported positive
numbers.

The immediately previous narrative is better at evidence boundaries, closest
work, fair comparisons, and honest negative results. It is worse at preserving
the user's scientific objective: it makes hierarchy comparison the headline
and lets local negative experiments redefine what the paper is about.

The accepted narrative is stronger and more faithful because it keeps the
initial problem and two-object mechanism while importing the previous version's
evidentiary discipline. It is ambitious as a hypothesis and cautious only in
what results are claimed as achieved. Newness alone played no role in the
decision.

## Evidence And Alternatives

The artifact already supports operation ingestion, mapping, query predicates,
stack folding, weighted views, and standard profiler outputs. Existing results
establish reach and conservation. AgentRx/TELBench invalidate the tested
positive leaf-localization claim. Hodoscope establishes a sparse-anomaly
boundary and rules out automatic recursive advantage. None of these results
tests a directly recorded additive regression recurring across source trees.

Rejected alternatives are: shrinking to pprof serialization; presenting a
universal semantic-hierarchy win; making representation sensitivity the center
before repeated matched experiments require it; or adding a selector, graph,
scope tree, contract, and large comparator tournament. Representation
sensitivity remains a reversible competing branch if several proper additive
experiments show no semantic advantage.
