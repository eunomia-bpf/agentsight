differential flame graphs, Pivot Tracing, and aggregate distributed-trace work

## Meaning Of Existing Results

The local corpus establishes normalization, reprojection, and mass
conservation, not correct semantic lineage or better decisions. Mapping results
show uneven field transfer, not latent-intent correctness. AgentRx/TELBench show
that the tested semantic leaves do not manufacture missing failure signal.
Hodoscope shows that a strong continuous semantic bundle dominates the tested
recursive stack for a sparse action oracle and that adding parents provides no
stable matched advantage. These are mechanism and workload boundaries, not a
direct refutation of cross-run profiling for measured additive effects.

RQ1 still lacks independent lineage, RQ2 lacks a decisive positive or
predictive additive-signal condition, and RQ3 lacks unchanged transfer of a
complete stack and decision protocol. The paper is scientifically interesting
but incomplete for a top venue.

## Next Decisive Experiment

Test one hypothesis under RQ2: when an additive regression is distributed over
recurring semantic roles in many source trees, a fixed semantic operation-stack
profile reduces effort to locate the independently documented change relative
to flat and genuine source-native views under matched information and cost.

Use a real agent and published benchmark, preferably a documented version or
configuration regression. Record genuine run/span/tool relationships, additive
tokens/time/retries/tool calls/system effects, task and trial identity,
benchmark outcomes, and identifiers that connect the documented change to
observed operations. Fix the semantic mapping on separate development evidence.
Run only three views over the same operations: flat, genuine native, and one
unchanged semantic stack. The primary outcome should use raw operations or
characters inspected before reaching independently implicated behavior, with
complete tasks, trials, repetitions, failures, and nonterminations retained.

A semantic win supports the recurring-distributed hypothesis; a native win
supports native sufficiency; a flat win supports grouping- or information-only
explanations; no difference means hierarchy is not consequential for that
regression. A gain that disappears under matched cost or requires target-time
stack choice is not evidence for the hierarchy.

## Larger Evidence Program

RQ1 later needs an independent native tool/span and process-lineage oracle.
RQ3 later needs unchanged transfer and tests of plain observable properties
such as recurrence, fragmentation, dispersion, and sparsity. The compelling
long-term result would be a compact empirical account in which isolated sparse
anomalies favor continuous flat search, subtree-local effects favor native
structure, and repeated distributed additive changes favor semantic folding.
These conditions should be accumulated through separate complete experiments,
not one huge matrix.
