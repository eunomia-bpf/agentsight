## Evidence And Claim Impact

Revision-0 raw evidence is admitted and negative. Across AgentRx, induced leaf
AP was 0.025841 versus prevalence 0.022358, with 20%-work recall 0.342466 and a
paired AP-minus-prevalence interval crossing zero. Across TELBench, induced AP
was 0.214872 versus prevalence 0.213843, with 20%-work recall 0.190047 and an
interval crossing zero. Matched comparisons failed the predeclared effect rule;
a TELBench width-only control was substantially stronger. Independent raw-result
reconstruction found no metric mismatch after correcting the per-trajectory
grouping audit.

This evidence invalidates positive claims based on flattened induced leaves. It
does not falsify the immutable RQ or the multi-resolution hierarchy because that
mechanism was not exercised. Revision 1 remains an untested ambitious hypothesis,
not a result. Its current experiment construct is unapproved until a later outer
cycle includes a mandatory complete-terminal-scope criterion with bounded
operation/token work and strong scope baselines.
