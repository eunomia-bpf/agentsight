# Iter-Refine-Ideas Round 2c — Independent Re-attack

## Setup

Because the team thread limit prevented a new thread, an independent reviewer
that had not participated in the Round-2 attack/defense reread the current paper,
human intent, and admitted RQ2 evidence.  It was explicitly forbidden from
reading any `02-write-gate` reports.  It made no edits.

## Verdict

**REVISE.  The idea has not collapsed, but it is not hardened.**

The reviewer found a recoverable durable principle:

> Execution structure should not determine diagnostic responsibility.  Stable
> semantic identity can be propagated across heterogeneous intent and system
> events, retained at every hierarchy prefix, and navigated at the resolution
> demanded by the current analysis question.

The paper now honestly distinguishes accounting from causality and hierarchy
utility from relational representability.  However, its unique mechanism did
not yet follow from that principle.

## Must-fix idea defects

1. **The navigator was absent from Design.**  The formal view
   `(predicate, ordered fields, weight)` did not define a disjoint frontier,
   scope priority, whole-scope work budget, expansion, or termination.
2. **Manual and induced operation stacks appeared to be different objects.**
   The paper needed to state whether recursive temporal partitions become
   derived fields in the same formal model.
3. **“Scope before localization” is not novel.**  SDBL already defines and
   evaluates this two-stage pattern.  The defensible difference is a reusable
   cross-run, cross-layer accounting hierarchy used as the scope substrate.
4. **Cross-run profiling was disconnected from per-trace RQ2 localization.**
   A valid test must freeze semantic vocabulary and risk on development traces
   and transfer them to untouched families.
5. **Responsibility changed meaning at RQ2.**  The paper needed separate
   relations for accounting ownership and diagnostic correspondence.
6. **The negative leaf result did not uniquely isolate hierarchy.**  It only
   motivated hierarchy as the next mechanism hypothesis.
7. **The RQ set was not contribution-shaped.**  RQ1 was partly definitional and
   the mapping RQ validated only one identity backend.  The reviewer recommended
   merging mapping transfer into a stronger identity/inheritance RQ, retaining
   immutable RQ2, and using a third practicality RQ.
8. **“Query-conditioned” was ambiguous.**  Manual query-time selection and an
   algorithm whose topology/priority consumes the diagnostic query required
   different terms.

## Experiment gaps separated from idea gaps

The reviewer correctly classified fresh Who&When/TRAIL execution, complete
release-pipeline cost, stronger SQL/native/matched/SDBL comparisons, and
cross-layer correlation validation as experiment obligations.  Their absence
prevents acceptance but does not require abandoning or narrowing the idea.

## AAAI assessment

Potentially suitable only if the complete navigator becomes an explicit method,
is differentiated from SDBL and SQL, and generalizes on fresh real benchmarks.
The current version remains a reject because its distinctive algorithm was
undefined and untested.

## Required next response

Revise the idea again, then perform another independent reattack.  Round 2 cannot
advance while the verdict remains `REVISE`.
