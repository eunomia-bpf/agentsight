## Blocking findings on entry

1. RQ1 truth needed exact units, denominators, candidate windows, merge/squash
   treatment, and zero/one/many labels. Naturalistic evidence cannot establish
   causal or authorial truth.
2. A confidence stratum needed preregistered held-out support, interval, and
   calibration criteria before it could gate RQ2/RQ3.
3. Path survival needed a continuous rename-aware lifetime rule; deletion and
   same-path recreation cannot count as continuous survival.

## Applied corrections

- Frozen the primary unit as an event--normalized-path pair and the target as a
  rename-aware file-change entry in a non-merge commit.
- Frozen a primary event-relative window of minus 15 minutes through plus 24
  hours on the same lifetime, with shorter windows only as prespecified
  sensitivity analyses.
- Defined separate zero, one, many, and unadjudicable labels and explicit
  denominators for candidate accuracy, top-k recall, unmatched specificity,
  and Git-side coverage. Squash and split histories do not force a bijection;
  merge changes are a separate stratum.
- Defined controlled truth as observed fixture correspondence and naturalistic
  truth as independently adjudicated evidence-compatible correspondence, never
  causality or authorship.
- Preregistered minimum held-out support, 95% Wilson lower bounds, and
  calibration limits for path and line strata. Failure to pass bounds the
  gallery to descriptive process/Git/mismatch/endpoint views.
- Defined a file lifetime as add through detected renames to deletion;
  same-path recreation starts a new lifetime identifier.

These corrections are synchronized across `docs/design.md`,
`docs/implementation.md`, `docs/evaluation.md`, and the paper. The paper still
compiles without undefined citations or references.
