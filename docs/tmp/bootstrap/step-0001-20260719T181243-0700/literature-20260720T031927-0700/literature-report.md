## Name-Free Candidate Claim

Under the same source evidence, supervisor model, prompt, and retrieval budget,
direct access to exact artifact effects across independent session and goal
boundaries helps an automatic supervisor diagnose and localize long-horizon
process pathologies more reliably than full-fidelity navigation of raw records.

## Findings

AggAgent establishes a competent coarse-to-fine interface over full-fidelity
long trajectories through lexical search and exact segment reads. A static raw
dump, summary-only condition, or truncated context is therefore not the main
baseline. AgentTether establishes Transition Units, a dependency-aware Critical
Transition Graph, learned/run-local graph diagnosis, repair memory, and runtime
intervention. OCEL 2.0 formally establishes time-stamped events, uniquely
identified objects, event-to-object and object-to-object relations, qualifiers,
and time-varying object attributes.

Consequently, the project cannot claim graph construction, event/object
lifecycle representation, temporal graph traversal, or Agent-operated raw-log
navigation as novelty. The surviving empirical question is whether exact,
source-linked persistent-workspace effects across independent sessions/goals
change automatic supervisory performance at a matched resource budget.

## Baseline Handoff

The strongest main baseline remains AggAgent-style Full Raw Retrieval over the
identical source store. It represents the competing scientific position that a
capable supervisor can recover all necessary evidence from complete raw records
without a workspace relation index. If it ties or wins on diagnosis and evidence
localization, the stronger representation claim is rejected; if it ties but is
materially more expensive, only an efficiency claim remains.

Reduced controls are Final State/Outcome, Native Report or frozen session
summaries, Counts, State Diff, and Target-Only/Session-Local access. Compatible
external comparisons are AgentTether for graph-guided failed-run diagnosis,
AgentRx and TrajAudit for failure localization, OCPM features for lifecycle
process analysis, and Full HTIR for harness-failure slices. They should not be
forced into the two-episode mechanics preflight or compared numerically outside
compatible inputs and outputs.

## Ad-Hoc Risks And Experiment Implications

The deterministic ownership and lifecycle projection is principled only if its
soundness, unresolved rate, and coverage are measured independently. The three
convenience queries are still exposed to feature-selection criticism. Formalize
a small label-independent relation algebra, compile the queries from it, and
give Raw exact metadata filtering so the comparison is relation computation
rather than poor search UX. Sweep evidence budgets and ablate cross-goal
history, order, lifecycle, and system binding. Preserve natural held-out
episodes and independent labels alongside controlled perturbations.
