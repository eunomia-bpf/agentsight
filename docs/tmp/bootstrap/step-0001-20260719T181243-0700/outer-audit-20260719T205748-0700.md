## EXPERIMENT_GATE audit

### What passed

1. **User-intent fidelity.** The work preserved action time rather than commit
   time, retained coding and non-coding workspaces, made visualization auxiliary,
   and selected an automatic supervisor agent as the evaluated consumer.
2. **Closest-work pressure.** Primary papers and official artifacts correctly
   ruled out generic trajectory diagnosis, generic cross-session novelty,
   generic harness diagnosis, and online intervention as standalone claims.
3. **Real local trace contact.** The existing session/visualization path ran on
   multiple real workspaces and exposed a genuine `--global` selection defect.
   Excluding line-selected global sessions from diagnosis is justified.
4. **Ambition preservation.** The work did not retreat to visualization quality,
   coding-only counts, or an efficiency claim before evidence. Raw Retrieval and
   Counts remain live falsifiers, and non-coding generalization remains an RQ.
5. **No proxy overinterpretation.** Trace counts, media, dependency fixtures,
   tests, and repaired scorer cases were not presented as diagnosis outcomes.

### Blocking defects

#### 1. The declared experiment node did not complete

The RQ1 proposal is explicitly closed and returned after its third blocked
review. Later repairs do not establish approval, model engagement, valid labels,
or end-to-end feasibility. The correct route is a scientifically distinct node,
not a fourth review of the same proposal.

#### 2. Matched-access fairness is not executable as claimed

The paper promises matched returned token and byte limits, while the plan caps
tool calls and returned lines. One JSONL line can contain an arbitrarily large
result, so recording bytes after a run does not enforce equal access. A signed
manual command/path audit is also overbuilt and does not make arbitrary shell
retrieval scalable. Fairness belongs in the research interface and ordinary raw
outputs/result review.

#### 3. Baselines do not isolate workspace evolution

- A $W_0\rightarrow W_T$ workspace snapshot/diff baseline is absent. Final
  State exposes $W_T$, not the strongest non-temporal realized-change account.
- The RQ2 session-reset ablation does not substitute cleanly for a session-local
  or bounded aggregate reader when source search can reconstruct continuity.
- RQ3 centers harness-waste attribution but neither compares with HarnessFix nor
  records a concrete incompatibility argument.

A win could therefore reflect a weak state baseline, easier retrieval, or a
missing mechanism competitor rather than longitudinal workspace structure.

#### 4. Claim-support rules are narrower than RQ1

The positive rule requires pathology macro-F1 improvement and an evidence veto,
but not satisfactory intervention-recommendation performance. It also does not
require Workspace Trajectory to beat or remain competitive with all reduced
conditions before claiming improvement over them. The output is mostly a Boolean
decision plus earliest action; the paper must retain that narrow output or plan
evidence for broader redirect/stop/clarify/repair recommendations.

#### 5. Label and workload feasibility remains untested

The 40-episode coding sample may contain too few positives for one or more fixed
pathologies. Dropping under-supported labels could let a four-pathology claim
rest on a prevalent subset. No prevalence/power or enrichment rule prevents
that outcome. Likewise, no real-path check establishes compatible capture, goal
boundaries, label coverage, or one taxonomy on persistent non-coding workspaces.

#### 6. Adjacent-mechanism grounding remains open

The literature report leaves object-/artifact-centric process mining unsearched,
although multi-object events, artifact lifecycle, transitions, and conformance
are close to the proposed mechanism. Generic Agent-diagnosis novelty is grounded;
the mechanism and baseline family are not yet ready to freeze.

### Raw-evidence qualification

The literature node links primary sources and retained PDFs. The data audit has
reconstructable commands, named workspaces, and a revision, but its aggregate
outputs were temporary rather than retained raw artifacts. They remain useful
feasibility observations, not paper evidence.

### EXPERIMENT_GATE disposition

**FAIL phase-exit verification.** Preserve the completed literature and trace
branches; re-enter through a new experiment node.
