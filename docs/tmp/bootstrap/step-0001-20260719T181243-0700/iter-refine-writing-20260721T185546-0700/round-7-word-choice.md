# Round 7 — Word Choice and Precision

## Independent review

Reviewer: `writing_round7_words` (read-only), using the systems-paper prose
checklist.

The audit found abbreviation drift between Raw/Trajectory and the registered
condition names, vague uses of “utility,” evaluative adjectives such as
“capable,” ambiguous current-versus-future repair language, and several weak or
colloquial phrases.

## Applied changes

- defined `Full Raw Retrieval (Full Raw)` and consistently named Workspace
  Trajectory in prose while retaining short subscripts in formulas;
- reserved “objective intervention utility” for the RQ1 claim and replaced
  weaker phrases such as representation value, supervisory utility, and
  treatment utility;
- described the automatic outcome as an executable continuation score and
  removed the unmeasured adjective “capable” from the supervisor;
- replaced semantic-gold wording with semantic-diagnosis wording and specified
  that no human annotations, agent-generated labels, or LLM-judge scores enter
  the outcome;
- named the actual four Full Raw operations and three Workspace Trajectory
  relation queries;
- restricted the mandatory evidence-engagement gate to Full Raw and Workspace
  Trajectory, the two historical-evidence conditions;
- made the completed-run, repaired-harness, and future-run tenses explicit;
- tightened repeated status and design prose to preserve the six-page length;
  and
- corrected sentence-initial capitalization and other local grammar defects.

## Validation

- registered RQs, estimands, conditions, task scores, budgets, and negative
  findings remain unchanged;
- LaTeX compile: PASS;
- compiled length: 6 pages;
- undefined citations/references: none reported.
