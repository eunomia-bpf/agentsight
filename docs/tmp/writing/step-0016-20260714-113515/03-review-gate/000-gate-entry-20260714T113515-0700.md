# REVIEW Gate Entry — Milestone Review 001

**Entered:** 2026-07-14 11:35:15 -0700

## Question

Given the complete current AAAI-format paper and all evidence accumulated through Step 0015, is the paper scientifically ready to submit, and if not, what is the single highest-paper-value next action?

## Review Method

- First read the complete paper without prior project memory or external search.
- Then search primary sources for closest systems, AI-agent observability, profiling, and failure-attribution work and verify AAAI requirements.
- Reread the complete paper after the source search and compare every major concern with the paper's actual evidence.
- Audit what changed in this cycle and whether any repository-local capability needs improvement.
- Preserve the fixed thesis and four RQs; experiment-level results answer only their tested hypothesis.
- Prefer reuse of existing public data, artifacts, protocols, and official evaluations. Reject new infrastructure or broad reruns unless they clearly change a paper-level answer.

## Exit Condition

The gate exits after the four review reports and a fresh independent outer audit cover the complete paper, external evidence, user intent, cycle changes, and one concrete routing decision. Review need not eliminate every scientific objection; it must distinguish submission blockers from bounded limitations and select a proportionate next action.
