### Two identities

An online task-instance ID records a particular push or replacement occurrence.
It supports controller state, occurrence lineage, and contiguous stage
segmentation. A profiling semantic identity is the visible stack-label path
folded across samples. The paper's task-semantic view needs the latter for
aggregation even if the controller internally keeps the former.

CodeTrace stages distinguish contiguous occurrences within a session; they do
not define cross-run semantic responsibility classes. Ordinary B-cubed over
session-namespaced visible paths may test within-session stage fidelity, but it
cannot validate global recurring responsibility, ancestor topology, variable
depth, generated-label meaning, or the lower
`phase/strategy -> action -> object -> result` suffix.
