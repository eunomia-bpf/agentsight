## Must-Fix Findings

1. The entry plan attributed a possible gain only to removing numeric tokens,
   but it changed both representation and transition policy by adding explicit
   `stay`/`switch`. The causal claim had to cover the complete interface bundle
   or add another arm.
2. The rejected numeric candidate should be a paired representation/control;
   multi-resolution recurrence is the sole mechanism adoption baseline.
3. The plan needed actual commands, fixed runtime, raw/result paths, execution
   completion conditions, and a realistic runtime estimate.
4. Every paper-facing standard metric needed its defining literature precedent
   and exact aggregation rule.
5. A positive result on the reused population must remain post-hoc development
   evidence rather than being called a validated or confirmatory component.

## Repairs

- Narrowed the question and hypothesis to the full **index-free semantic
  transition interface**. The plan no longer attributes a result solely to
  numeric-token removal.
- Made multi-resolution recurrence the one adoption comparator. The numeric
  candidate now supplies a separate paired diagnostic that cannot authorize
  adoption or prove a single cause.
- Added exact llama.cpp, inference, and scoring commands; fixed model/runtime,
  paths, expected duration, and explicit completion counts.
- Defined pooled exact-span, ordinary unweighted B-cubed, and exact adjacent-
  boundary metrics and cited Black et al. 1991, Bagga and Baldwin 1998, Amigó
  et al. 2009, and Fournier 2013.
- Restricted any positive outcome to bounded post-hoc development evidence on
  the reused trajectories.

No experiment arm, benchmark, feature, cutoff, custom metric, paper claim, or
extra gate was added.
