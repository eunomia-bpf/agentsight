## Experiment Result

The TraceElephant tested hypothesis closes as `VALID / COMPLETE /
INCONCLUSIVE`. At the predeclared 80% macro recall point, AgentProf needs
100.00% work versus 71.91% for raw action; the AgentProf-minus-raw 95% interval
is `[-1.90, +45.86]` percentage points. All matched permutations do no worse
than the actual semantic assignment.

The curve is not uniformly negative. AgentProf reaches 50% macro recall at
19.55% work versus 46.64% for raw action and reaches 52.57% recall at about 20%
work versus 23.79% for raw. The predeclared high-recall failure is caused by a
large final tied tier. It bounds this propagation/ranking construction and does
not challenge the thesis, RQ2, or semantic profiling program.
