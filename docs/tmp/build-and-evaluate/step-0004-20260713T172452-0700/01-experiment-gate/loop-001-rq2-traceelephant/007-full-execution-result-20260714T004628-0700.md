## Primary Result

At the approved threshold of at least 80% macro decisive-step recall:

| Method | Atomic-step work | Relevant achieved recall |
|---|---:|---:|
| AgentProf semantic operation stack | 1.0000 | 1.0000 macro at final tied tier |
| Source-native raw action | 0.7191 | 0.8539 macro |
| AgentProf minus raw | +0.2809 | higher is worse |

The paired 95% bootstrap interval for AgentProf-minus-raw work is
`[-0.0190, +0.4586]`. It crosses zero, so the approved rule does not classify
the construction as reliably contradicted. However, the point estimate is in
the wrong direction and therefore cannot support the tested hypothesis.

The matched semantic-permutation control also fails its engagement criterion:
all 200 permutations require no more 80%-recall work than the actual semantic
assignment, giving `p=1.0` rather than the required `1/201`. Thus the fixed
role/intent/status alignment did not supply the planned advantage on this
population.

## Curve Shape And Controls

The failure is not simply an everywhere-flat profile:

- AgentProf reaches 50% macro recall at 19.55% work, versus 46.64% for raw.
- At nominal 20% work, AgentProf has 52.57% macro recall, versus 23.79% for raw.
- The AgentProf curve then reaches only 73.35% macro recall at 63.49% work and
  must consume the final tied tier to reach the 80% threshold, jumping to full
  work.
- Width-only grouping reaches 80% macro recall at 67.68% work, better than both
  the fixed AgentProf score and the raw headline baseline at this threshold.
- The scorer-only oracle reaches the threshold at 3.69% work, confirming that
  the metric can express a strongly useful ranking.
- Session and source-native per-run controls both require full work at the 80%
  threshold, so merely retaining execution identity does not solve the task.

These descriptive results suggest a specific mechanism problem: the current
semantic stack creates useful early concentration but the shared localizer-hit
prefix score leaves a large, decision-critical tail insufficiently separated.
The matched permutations further indicate that the fixed semantic tag
alignment is not the source of the early advantage. This is a diagnosis to be
audited, not authorization to retune on these 220 labels.
