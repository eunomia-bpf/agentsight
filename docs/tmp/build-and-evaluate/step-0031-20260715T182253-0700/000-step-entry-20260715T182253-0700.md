## Why Another Experiment Is Being Considered

RQ1, RQ2, and RQ4 have positive paper-level answers. RQ3 has positive public
evidence for task partitions and operation-group boundaries, including the
current built-in recurrence mechanism, but not for literal task names. Another
boundary cutoff, score, or benchmark would repeat the already answered part of
the RQ. The only admissible experiment in this step is therefore one that:

1. tests literal task identity rather than another partition-only score;
2. reuses an existing published real-agent benchmark in full;
3. changes the existing tagger minimally instead of inventing another
   constructor;
4. keeps target labels out of inference; and
5. can change the cumulative RQ3 answer and the AAAI reviewer assessment.

The root first performs a source-fidelity screen. A candidate that lacks paired
visible task text and an independent official identity is recorded and skipped;
the project does not substitute a convenient proxy merely to produce a result.
